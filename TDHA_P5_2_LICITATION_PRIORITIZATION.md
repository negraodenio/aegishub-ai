## 3. In-Depth Analysis of P5.2 Candidates

### 3.1 Context Recovery / Decompression Flow (Score: 93.5 — TIER 1)
- **Description:** A guided 2-minute cognitive re-entry sequence triggered after interruptions, long meetings, or mental context switching. It asks: *"Where did you leave off?"*, provides the last completed action, and guides 3 deep breaths before showing the single next micro-step.
- **Licitation Value:** Public tenders for public administration and healthcare professionals place huge emphasis on reducing cognitive switching errors and burnout.
- **Privacy & Safety:** 100% private to the employee. Zero employer telemetry beyond anonymous aggregate count ($N \ge 20$).

### 3.2 Adaptive Energy-Match Prioritization (Score: 91.0 — TIER 1)
- **Description:** Integrates the employee's current energy rating (1-10) with task complexity. When energy is $\le 4$, the workspace highlights low-friction tasks ("Answer 1 email", "File expense receipts"). When energy is $\ge 7$, it surfaces high-focus strategic tasks.
- **Licitation Value:** Matches European OSHA (EU-OSHA) psychosocial risk reduction guidelines by aligning workload demands with real-time human capacity.
- **Privacy & Safety:** Purely algorithmic local sorting. No energy ratings are ever exposed individually.

### 3.3 Meeting Preparation & Action Offloader (Score: 88.0 — TIER 1)
- **Description:** Allows employees to paste an overwhelming meeting agenda, calendar invite, or chaotic notes. The LLM produces: (1) 3 Key Objectives to focus on, (2) 2 Questions to ask, and (3) Immediate post-meeting checklist items.
- **Licitation Value:** Directly demonstrable in enterprise sales demos and public sector executive workflows.
- **Privacy & Safety:** Passed through pre-flight PII scrubber and hashed via HMAC audit trail.

### 3.4 Scientific MEQ-5 Chronotype Profiler (Score: 87.5 — TIER 1)
- **Description:** Embeds the scientifically validated 5-item Morningness-Eveningness Questionnaire (Adan & Almirall, 1991). Calculates the employee's chronotype (Lion/Morning, Bear/Intermediate, Wolf/Evening, Dolphin/Variable) to recommend optimal personal focus windows.
- **Licitation Value:** Gives the platform rigorous academic credibility grounded in peer-reviewed chronobiology without making clinical claims.

### 3.5 Checklist & Corporate SOP Generator (Score: 84.0 — TIER 2)
- **Description:** Generates structured, step-by-step checklists for recurring complex corporate workflows (e.g., onboarding a new vendor, submitting quarterly reports, closing sprint tickets).
- **Licitation Value:** Valuable for ISO 9001 process compliance and operational continuity.
